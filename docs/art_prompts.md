# Art prompts

Prompts for turning Charlie's drawings into game art (Gemini image
generation). Attach the named drawing to each. The originals stay in
`drawings/`; finished art goes in `assets/`.

Every prompt asks for a plain white background so Claude can cut the
character out cleanly, and asks the tool to stay faithful to Charlie's
shapes rather than redesign them.

## Helper 1 (blue) — attach `drawings/helper1_blue.png`

```
Turn the attached child's drawing into a clean, colourful 2D cartoon character for a children's video game. Stay faithful to the drawing: keep the same shapes, the same body parts in the same places, the same face and happy expression, and the same simple charm. Do not add extra details, clothes, accessories, props or text. Use smooth, thick dark outlines and flat bright colours with only a little simple shading. Show the full body, front view, centred, filling most of the picture with a small margin around it. Plain pure white background: no ground, no shadow, no scenery, no text. Square image.

This is Helper 1, a friendly creature coloured bright blue. Its round, egg-shaped blue body is also its head. It has two small dot eyes, a small round nose and a big happy smile, one curly hair on top, two small rounded arms sticking out at the sides like little ears, and two short oval feet.
```
