# Pitch: Charlie's game (no name yet)

From Charlie's first interview, 2026-09-26 (10 questions, Gareth typing).
Charlie's own words are in quotes; everything else is a choice he picked.

## The game in one line
You fight baddies and dragons in a big city with a sword and a hammer, and
collect the gems they leave behind.

## Charlie's answers
1. **Who you are:** "A boy or a girl". **You pick at the start.**
2. **Where:** a big city.
3. **What you do most:** "Fight".
4. **What you pick up:** "Theres a gem which every fight drops and you've
   got to collect them".
5. **Who you fight:** "different types of enemies and dragons!"
6. **How you fight:** "Sword and hammer".
7. **Gems:** you **just count them**. Getting lots is the fun.
8. **Beating a baddie:** "they sink into the ground".
9. **Getting hit:** you **lose a heart**.

## Keep it right for a 5-year-old
Cartoon fighting only: bonks and swooshes, no blood, nothing scary. Baddies
sink into the ground rather than anything grim. The game must play without
reading: hearts and a gem counter shown as pictures and numbers.

## Gareth's decisions (2026-09-26)
- **A side-scrolling beat-'em-up** where you can also walk up and down the
  street (a "belt" like Streets of Rage). Charlie has played and loved
  Power Rangers: Rita's Rewind, which is the model to follow.
- **Controls:** keyboard, plus the Xbox and Switch controllers the family
  already has.
- **Out of hearts:** restart the fight, and keep your gems.
