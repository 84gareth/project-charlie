# Pitch: Charlie's game (no name yet)

From Charlie's first interview, 2026-09-26 (10 questions, Gareth typing).
Charlie's own words are in quotes; everything else is a choice he picked.

## The game in one line
You fight baddies and dragons in a big city with a sword and a hammer, and
collect the gems they leave behind.

## Charlie's answers
1. **Who you are:** "A boy or a girl". **You pick at the start.**
2. **Where:** a big city.
3. **What you do most:** "Fight".
4. **What you pick up:** "Theres a gem which every fight drops and you've
   got to collect them".
5. **Who you fight:** "different types of enemies and dragons!"
6. **How you fight:** "Sword and hammer".
7. **Gems:** you **just count them**. Getting lots is the fun.
8. **Beating a baddie:** "they sink into the ground".
9. **Getting hit:** you **lose a heart**.

## More from Charlie (2026-09-26, second chat)
- **The sword:** "you have a microphone and tell the sword what to do".
  The microphone is in the game, not a real one. What do you tell it? "you
  tell the sword whatever you want to".
- **The hammer:** "you do a spin hammer attack".
- **Islands and bosses:** "Theres different islands and on each island is a
  boss and if you beat the boss you get the gems. You defeat every single
  one to reach the final boss."
- **Who gives gems?** Charlie picked **every baddie** (beat one, get a
  gem). Boss gems can come with the islands.
- **How many hearts?** "12".
- **The girl hero's hair:** two pigtails.
- **Whole touch test (v0.6.0):** "It's great." Charlie wants to draw
  things to go in the game.
- **Helpers (2026-09-27):** creatures "that help you in battle (like
  Pokemon type creatures)". Charlie wants to draw them himself. What do
  they do? They **attack** and **give you hearts**.
- **The three helpers (drawn 2026-09-27, `drawings/helpers_all.png`):**
  level 1 to 3, left to right. "You need to get different amount of gems
  to get them. 200, 500 and 700. They use their powers to defeat the
  enemies." Colours: level 1 **blue**, level 2 **yellow**, level 3
  **green**.
  - Level 1 (`drawings/helper1_blue.png`): a round egg-shaped body with a
    smiley face, a curl of hair, little round arms and two feet.
  - Level 2 (`drawings/helper2_yellow.png`): a round face, stick arms and
    legs, and a tall zig-zag lightning shape on its head.
  - Level 3 (`drawings/helper3_green.png`): arms up high, legs wide, a
    beard, and a big scribbly shape all around it.
  - **Reach, not spend:** at 200 gems the blue helper comes and "I keep
    my gems".
  - **Who comes:** "All of them!"
  - **Powers:** "blue - has waves, yellow has sneaky powers and green has
    flying powers".
  - **Gem value (Gareth):** each gem is worth 10, keeping Charlie's
    200/500/700 (blue after about 7 waves).
  - Art: Charlie's drawings → Gemini (`drawings/helper*_gemini.jpg`) →
    Meshy 3D (`drawings/models/*.glb`) → rendered sprites in `assets/`.
- **First play (v0.4.0, fight mode):** "super fun".

## Charlie's three baddies (drawn 2026-10-25)
Charlie's own baddies, to replace the purple placeholder monsters.
- **The house bug** (`drawings/baddie_house_bug.jpg`): a black body, two
  blue striped wings, five dangly tentacles. "it can fly and it uses its
  tentacles to capture your character."
- **The Acorn bad guy** (`drawings/baddie_acorn.jpg`; Charlie drew it
  upside down, the original is `baddie_acorn_as_drawn.jpg`): a brown acorn
  with a dark cap, two wings and five legs. "They drop from trees and
  attack you and can fly too."
- **The teleportation bug** (`drawings/baddie_teleport_bug.jpg`): a long
  purple body with a dark band at the top and two purple arms. "it can
  teleport anywhere to scare you, behind, above and in front of you!"
- **Getting away from the house bug:** "There will be a button you need to
  tap three times to get free" (Charlie will draw the button).
- **Gareth (2026-10-25):** the bugs come in Phase 4, step 3, in place of
  the purple placeholder monsters: the same fight first, then their
  special moves one at a time. Simple shapes stand in until their Gemini
  and Meshy pictures are made.

## Charlie's mini game: ball and goal (drawn 2026-10-25)
"This is the goal and ball. You have to kick the ball into it as a mini
game you can do whenever you want." Drawings: `drawings/minigame_ball.jpg`
(a black ball) and `drawings/minigame_goal.jpg` (a net). Not yet on the
roadmap: waiting for Gareth's OK on where it goes.

## Keep it right for a 5-year-old
Cartoon fighting only: bonks and swooshes, no blood, nothing scary. Baddies
sink into the ground rather than anything grim. The game must play without
reading: hearts and a gem counter shown as pictures and numbers.

## Gareth's decisions (2026-09-26)
- **A side-scrolling beat-'em-up** where you can also walk up and down the
  street (a "belt" like Streets of Rage). Charlie has played and loved
  Power Rangers: Rita's Rewind, which is the model to follow.
- **Controls:** keyboard, plus the Xbox and Switch controllers the family
  already has.
- **Out of hearts:** restart the fight, and keep your gems.
